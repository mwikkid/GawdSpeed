GawdSpeed Practice & transcription player · Guide

Slow a song down without changing its key, loop the hard part, and work it out by ear. Hold the pointer over any control for a tooltip.

  1. Open… a song or video (⌘O), or drag a file onto the window: WAV, MP3, FLAC, AIFF, M4A, OGG, Opus, MP4, MOV and more.
  2. Link field. Paste a YouTube, SoundCloud, Bandcamp or Vimeo link and press Return (⌘U jumps here). The audio goes to Music ▸ GawdSpeed ▸ Downloads. The first time, GawdSpeed sets up the free yt-dlp tool it uses.
  3. Regions. Named sections of this song, like “Bridge lick”. Highlight a section and save it here (⌘D); later, jump back to it or loop it. They show as orange bars on the overview.
  4. Algorithm A | B. Two ways of slowing audio down. Try both: one may sound better on a given song.
  5. Overview. The whole song. The box shows what the big waveform is showing; drag it to move around.
  6. Waveform. Click to jump; drag across it to highlight a section (a click outside the highlight clears it). Pinch, ⌘-scroll or ⌘+/⌘− to zoom; scroll to move. Right-click for more.
  7. The highlight. Drag its edges, or its middle, to adjust it. Edges snap to where the wave crosses zero, which keeps loops click-free.
  8. Transport. Back to start (or the loop’s start), back 5 s, play / pause (Space), ahead 5 s, and where you are in the song, in the song’s own time whatever the speed.
  9. Loop, In, Out, ✕. Loop repeats the highlight (L). I and O set the start and end at the playhead, or click a time to type one. ✕ (or Esc) clears the highlight.
  10. Export… (⌘E) saves the slowed-down audio as a new file (the whole song or the highlight) as WAV, AIFF, FLAC, ALAC, AAC or MP3. The original is never changed.
  11. Speed. 25% to 100%, and the key stays the same. −/= change it by 5% (⌥ for 1%); 50, 75 and 100 jump straight there.
  12. transpose and tune. Change the key without changing the speed: transpose in semitones ([/]); tune in cents, for recordings slightly sharp or flat (⌥[/⌥]).
  13. original key. Appears only while the key is shifted. Click it (or ⌘0) to go back.
  14. High-pass and low-pass. High-pass cuts bass (turn it up); low-pass cuts treble (turn it down). Drag or scroll; double-click to switch off. Handy for picking out a bass line or a hi-hat part.

Your first loop, in 30 seconds

  1. Drag a song onto the window.
  2. Click 75 (or drag the speed slider).
  3. Drag across the hard part of the waveform to highlight it.
  4. Press L to loop it, then Space to play.
  5. When it’s comfortable, nudge the speed up with =.

GawdSpeed remembers each song: speed, key, filters, loop, regions and where you were come back when you open it again. ⌘Z undoes changes to the controls and the loop.

Good to know

At 100% speed with no key change, GawdSpeed plays the original audio untouched. Only download audio you have the right to use. Help ▸ Show Tips replays the first-run tips.
